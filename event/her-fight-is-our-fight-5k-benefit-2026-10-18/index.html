Her Fight is Our Fight 5K The Her Fight is Our Fight 5K benefits Officer Kimberly Del Angel as she courageously battles breast cancer . Officer Del Angel has dedicated her life to serving and protecting our community , both in and out of uniform. She is the kind of person who always puts others before herself. Her compassion , unwavering faith , and selfless heart have touched countless lives throughout the Rio Grande Valley. Now it's our turn to stand beside her. By signing up for the Her Fight is Our Fight 5K , you're doing more than participating in a race—you're becoming part of a community that refuses to let one of its own fight alone. Every registration helps ease the financial burden of medical expenses while reminding Officer Del Angel that she is surrounded by people who love , support , and believe in her.
