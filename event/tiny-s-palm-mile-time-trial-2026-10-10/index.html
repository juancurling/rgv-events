Tiny's Palm Mile - Time Trial

Saturday, October 10, 2026 · 7:30 AM

266 Mexico Blvd., Brownsville, TX, 78520

5K / 10K Runs & Races$0.00

This race is FREE and staged to prepare runners for the 30th running of Tiny's Palm Mile on Saturday, November 7, 2026. The recorded chip times will be used to position runners for the November race. “Let’s See What You Got!” Your time will be recorded and the challenge is yours. Train the month of October and try to beat your time in November. No Cost, No shirts, No Awards. Just a Time Trial! Just for Fun! History: The Palm Mile is back after 10 years of dormancy. Come join us to celebrate Alonso “Tiny” Barrientes, founding father of the Brownsville Marathoners & a Vietnam Purple Heart recipient. Let's all enjoy the Brownsville Marathoner’s 50th Anniversary as your running club!
