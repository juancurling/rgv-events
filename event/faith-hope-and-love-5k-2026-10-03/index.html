Faith • Hope • Love 5K Run/Walk for a Cure Every Step Makes a Difference Join the Weslaco Running Club for the Faith • Hope • Love 5K Run/Walk for a Cure , a community event dedicated to supporting Maria Jiron as she courageously battles breast cancer. Maria is much more than a patient—she is a beloved daughter, sister, cousin, mother, wonderful grandmother, treasured friend, and valued coworker whose kindness has touched countless lives. Now it's our turn to give back. Whether you run for a personal best, walk alongside family and friends, or simply want to make a difference, your participation matters . Every registration helps provide financial support for Maria and reminds her that she is not fighting alone.
