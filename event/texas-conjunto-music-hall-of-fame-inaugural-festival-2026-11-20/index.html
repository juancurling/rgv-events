Texas Conjunto Music Hall of Fame Inaugural Festival

Friday, November 20, 2026 · 12:00 AM

Stookey Park · San Benito, TX

Nonprofit & Community$10 per day

Two-day inaugural conjunto music festival at Stookey Park in San Benito, the birthplace of conjunto music and hometown of Freddy Fender and Charley Crockett. Lineup highlights female conjunto artists and living legends. Proceeds support the Texas Conjunto Music Hall of Fame and Museum's operations and education programs. Daily start times were not announced.
