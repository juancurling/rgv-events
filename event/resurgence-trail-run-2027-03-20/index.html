Rise. Renew. Resurge. Experience the beauty of the Rio Grande Valley at the Resurgence Trail Run on March 20, 2027, at Arroyo Park in Harlingen, Texas. Journey through scenic trails shaded by native trees, embrace the challenge of winding singletrack, and reconnect with the outdoors alongside an inspiring community of runners. Whether you're taking your first steps into trail running or returning stronger than ever, Resurgence is more than a race—it's a celebration of resilience, growth, and new beginnings. Every mile is an opportunity to leave yesterday behind and discover what you're truly capable of. This spring, answer the call of the trail and experience your own resurgence.
