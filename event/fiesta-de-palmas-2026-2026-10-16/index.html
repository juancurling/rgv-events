Fiesta de Palmas 2026

Friday, October 16, 2026 · 5:00 PM

McAllen Convention Center · 700 Convention Center Blvd., McAllen, TX 78501

City EventsFree Friday; $10 after 4 p.m. Saturday and Sunday★ Sponsorship / vendor opportunities🏪 Booth space available

The City of McAllen's three-day fall festival at the McAllen Convention Center. Friday Oct. 16 5-11 p.m. (free admission), Saturday Oct. 17 3-11 p.m. and Sunday Oct. 18 3-10 p.m. ($10 after 4 p.m.). Features an expanded carnival with 30+ rides, an H-E-B Helping Here pumpkin patch, a Dia de los Muertos altar, mariachi competition, costume contest, STEAM activities, artisan market, fireworks and headliners La Santa Cecilia and Y La Bamba. Sponsors include H-E-B, L&F Distributors and McAllen International Airport. Vendor/artisan mercado booths on-site.
