Bats & Bases 5K The Bats & Bases 5K is a fun, family-friendly community run celebrating two things we love—Halloween and softball! This 5K brings together runners, walkers, families, athletes, and supporters for a morning of fitness, fun, and community spirit. Whether you’re a seasoned runner, a casual walker, a softball or baseball fan, or simply looking for a fun way to start your morning, the Bats & Bases 5K is for you! Come run, walk, cheer, and celebrate with us as we hit the pavement and knock it out of the park! All proceeds will support the Edinburg High School Softball program, helping provide opportunities, resources, and support for our student-athletes.
