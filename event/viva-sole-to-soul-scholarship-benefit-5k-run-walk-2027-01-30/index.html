ViVa Sole to Soul Scholarship Benefit 5K Run/Walk

Saturday, January 30, 2027 · 8:00 AM

714 South Raul Longoria, Edinburg, TX, 78542

5K / 10K Runs & Races$15.00 - $25.00

As we kick off Black History Month, Village in The Valley is excited to present the 2nd Annual Sole to Soul 5K Run/Walk and 1-Mile Fun Run, a community event dedicated to supporting local students while celebrating unity, culture, and wellness! Date: Saturday, January 30th, 2027 Location: Edinburg Municipal Park - 714 South Raul Longoria Road, Edinburg, TX 78542 Time: 8:00-11:00AM All proceeds from this event will go toward the Village In The Valley Scholarship Fund, helping provide opportunities for deserving students to pursue higher education, ViVa’s initiatives, and programs that preserve and celebrate Black history in our community. By participating, you’re not just running or walking, you’re investing in the future of our community! Whether you run, walk, or cheer from the sidelines, your presence makes an impact! Come be part of a movement that celebrates unity, supports education
