“Pharr: Packed with History” Exhibit

Sunday, September 6, 2026 · 1:00 PM

Pharr Memorial Public Library – Galleria 121 · Pharr

More EventsFree

Free exhibit curated by the Museum of South Texas History (MOST History) for the City of Pharr, on display in Galleria 121, the multi-purpose room of the Pharr Memorial Public Library. Features roughly 90 Pharr-branded produce crate labels and black-and-white photographs of Pharr's early railroad and agricultural days. Per MyRGV.com the exhibit is on display every day through Sunday, Dec. 13, 2026. Library hours: Mon–Thu 9 a.m.–8:45 p.m.; Fri–Sat 9 a.m.–5:45 p.m.; Sun 1–5:45 p.m. Exhibit opened Sept. 2, 2026; start date here reflects the current collection date.
