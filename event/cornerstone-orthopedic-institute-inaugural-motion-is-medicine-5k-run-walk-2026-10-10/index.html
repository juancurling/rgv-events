Cornerstone Orthopedic Institute Inaugural Motion is Medicine 5k Run/Walk Saturday October 10, 2026 Cornerstone Orthopedic Institute Address: 2402 Cornerstone Blvd, Edinburg, TX In celebration of Bone and Joint Action Week, Cornerstone Orthopedic Institute will host its inaugural 5k Run/Walk to promote joint-friendly activity, prevention & awareness for orthopedic disorders such as trauma, spine diseases, arthritis, osteoporosis, and pediatric musculoskeletal conditions. After event expenses, all net proceeds will go directly toward helping patients in need by easing the burden of medical costs and making essential orthopedic care more accessible.
