25th Anniversary Remembrance of the Queen Isabella Causeway Collapse

Tuesday, September 15, 2026 · 11:00 AM

Port Isabel Event & Cultural Center · 309 E. Railroad Ave., Port Isabel, TX

City EventsFree

The City of Port Isabel hosts a memorial marking 25 years since the Sept. 15, 2001 Queen Isabella Causeway collapse that killed eight people. Program includes remarks from elected officials and community leaders, a U.S. Coast Guard color guard, a speaker panel on the collapse and rebuilding, and a screening of a TxDOT documentary.
