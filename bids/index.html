PlanHub setup (your subscription)

You already pay for PlanHub, so configure it as your PRIVATE/commercial-GC channel (most PlanHub volume is GC bid invitations on private and commercial work, not public agency postings):

1) COMPANY PROFILE & TRADES: Settings > Company Profile > Trades/Work Performed. Select your CSI divisions/sections (PlanHub supports multiple trade selections, up to ~15):
- Div 07 Thermal & Moisture Protection: 07 30 00 Steep Slope Roofing, 07 50 00 Membrane Roofing, 07 60 00 Flashing & Sheet Metal, 07 20 00 Thermal Protection (insulation), 07 80 00 Fire & Smoke Protection
- Div 09 Finishes: 09 20 00 Plaster & Gypsum Board (drywall), 09 50 00 Ceilings, 09 80 00 Acoustic Insulation
- Div 26 Electrical: 26 00 00 (all), incl. 26 50 00 Lighting
- Div 27 Communications: 27 10 00 Structured Cabling, 27 20 00 Data Communications (add Div 28 Electronic Safety & Security if you do access control/cameras)
- Structural: Div 05 Metals (05 10 00 Structural Metal Framing, 05 40 00 Cold-Formed Metal Framing) and Div 03 Concrete (03 30 00 Cast-in-Place) as applicable; add 06 10 00 Rough Carpentry if you frame.
Fully complete the profile (licenses, insurance, past projects, references) — GCs filter subs by profile completeness when auto-inviting.

2) SERVICE AREA / RADIUS: Settings > Service Area. Premier plans are radius-based from your business address (standard tiers 50/100/200 miles). For full RGV coverage, set HQ (e.g., McAllen or Pharr) with a 100-mile radius — that covers McAllen, Brownsville, Harlingen, Rio Grande City, and Raymondville. If your tier is smaller (e.g., ~75 miles), center it mid-Valley (Weslaco/Mercedes) so both the McAllen and Brownsville metro ends fall inside one circle.

3) PROJECT FEED FILTERS + SAVED SEARCH: In the Projects/Bid Board view, filter by your CSI divisions, location radius, bid due date, project stage, and construction type (check BOTH public and private — some RGV public work is reposted by GCs). Save the search so your feed defaults to it.

4) EMAIL ALERTS: Settings > Notifications. Turn ON: new projects matching my trades and area (instant or daily digest), bid invitations (GC ITBs), addenda/document updates, and GC messages. Alerts key off the trades and ZIP/radius set in steps 1-2, so wrong trade selections = missed invitations.

5) WORK THE NETWORK: Use the GC directory to connect with active RGV GCs (the same firms you'll meet through RGV AGC) so they add you to their private invite lists; keep your response rate high — PlanHub surfaces responsive subs to GCs.

6) REALITY CHECK: PlanHub will NOT reliably show City/County/ISD solicitations — those live on the IonWave/ProcureWare/OpenGov/BidNet/DemandStar portals listed above (all free vendor registration). Use PlanHub for commercial/private GC work and register on the public portals separately.
